Questions people ask first

Is Hello.Green free to use?

Yes. The calculators, guides and quote requests are free. We earn money from advertising, from partner installers when a homeowner asks for quotes, from clearly labelled sponsorships and from readers who choose to support us.

Will installers call me non-stop?

No. You choose how many pros (one to three) receive your request, and we only share it with partners who agree to contact you by the method you pick. You can ask us to withdraw your request at any time.

How accurate are the calculators?

They use published averages and the inputs you give. They are good for comparing options and spotting big wins. A site visit and a written quote are the only way to get an exact price for your home.

Do you cover my country?

The tools work anywhere: switch the currency and change the defaults. Our incentives directory covers the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, India and Australia, with more on the way.
