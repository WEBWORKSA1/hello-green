Partner standards

  • Licensed and insured as your region requires, with certifications where grants depend on them (for example MCS in the UK).
  • Verifiable reviews and at least one year of trading history.
  • Contact homeowners only by the method they chose, within two business days.
  • No high-pressure tactics, no same-day-only pricing, and written quotes that show equipment and warranties.

Partners that break these standards are removed from the network.
