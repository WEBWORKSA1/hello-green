Tools that turn “should I?” into a number

Each tool runs in your browser, remembers your inputs on this device and shows its assumptions. Change any default to match your bills.

  • Solar savings calculatorSizes a system from your bill, prices it with local defaults or your quote, applies rebates (including India’s PM Surya Ghar subsidy) and finds your payback year.Open
  • Carbon footprint calculatorQuick or detailed. Home energy, driving, flights, food, shopping and waste, compared with your country and with the 2-tonne climate goal.Open
  • Heat pump savingsWorks out the heat your home really uses from your current bill, then prices it through a heat pump at your electricity rate.Open
  • EV vs gas costFuel against home and public charging, maintenance, the price gap and CO₂.Open
  • Home energy quick winsLED bulbs, standby power, thermostat setbacks, water heater temperature and cold-water laundry, each with a yearly saving.Open
  • Recycling sorterSearch over 100 everyday items and see which bin they go in, with a tip for each.Open
  • Green Score quizTen questions, a score out of 100 and the three changes that would move you most.Open

Which tool should I start with?

If you own your home, start with the solar calculator and the heat pump calculator: they cover the two biggest money decisions most homeowners face. Renters get the most from quick wins and the footprint calculator.

Thinking about your next car? The EV calculator shows how much home charging changes the math.

Are the numbers reliable?

They are as good as the inputs. Defaults are typical household prices for each country and will not match your bill exactly. Replace them with your own rate per kWh and fuel price for a far better estimate. Every tool explains its method at the bottom of the page.
