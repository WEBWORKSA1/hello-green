Official rules

No purchase or payment is necessary to enter or win. A purchase will not improve your chances.

  1. Organiser. The contest is run by Hello.Green (“we”). Contact us through the contact page.
  2. Eligibility. Open to individuals aged 18 or older who are legal residents of the United States, Canada (excluding Québec), the United Kingdom, India or Australia. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Our staff, contractors, sponsors and their immediate families may not enter.
  3. Entry period. Entries must be received between 19 November 2026 and 11:59 pm Eastern Time on 31 January 2027. Late entries are not accepted.
  4. How to enter. Complete the entry form on this page. Limit one entry per person per category. Entries must be your own original work, must not infringe anyone’s rights, and must not contain anything unlawful, unsafe or offensive. We may disqualify entries that do.
  5. Judging. This is a contest of skill. A panel chosen by us will judge eligible entries on impact (40%), originality and ease of copying (30%) and clarity (30%). Judges’ decisions are final. Chance plays no part.
  6. Prizes. One first prize ($250), one second prize ($100) and one third prize ($50), in US dollars or local equivalent, awarded as a gift card or green-home products of equal value at our choice. Prizes are not transferable. We may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Approximate total prize value: $400.
  7. Winners. Potential winners will be contacted by email by 15 February 2027 and must reply within 14 days, or an alternate may be chosen. Residents of Canada must correctly answer a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question before being confirmed as a winner. Winners are responsible for any taxes on prizes.
  8. Publicity and licence. By entering, you allow us to publish your entry, first name and country on Hello.Green and our channels without further payment. You keep ownership of your work.
  9. Privacy. We use entry information only to run the contest and, if you opt in, to send our newsletter. See our privacy policy.
  10. General. We are not responsible for entries lost due to technical problems. We may cancel, suspend or modify the contest if fraud, technical failure or any other factor beyond our control affects its fairness, subject to applicable law. These rules are governed by the laws of the Province of Québec and the federal laws of Canada applicable there, except where the law of the winner’s place of residence requires otherwise.
