Real numbers for greener homes

Hello.Green helps households cut energy bills and carbon with free calculators, plain-English guides and an honest way to get quotes. We exist because the people selling upgrades should not be the only ones doing the math.

What we do

  • Tools: calculators for solar, heat pumps, EVs, home efficiency and carbon footprints that run in your browser and show their assumptions.
  • Guides: practical articles that explain costs, trade-offs and what to ask an installer.
  • Incentives: a directory of national and regional programs, with links to the official sources and the date we last checked.
  • Quotes: a free request form that connects homeowners with up to three vetted local pros.
  • Live data: green news, videos and climate indicators that update themselves every six hours.

How we make money

Being open about this is part of being trustworthy.

  • Advertising: display ads served by Google AdSense.
  • Installer partners: when you request quotes, the partner installers who receive your request pay us for the introduction. You pay nothing.
  • Sponsorships: brands can sponsor a tool, newsletter or contest. Sponsored content is always labelled.
  • Supporters: readers can contribute once or monthly to keep the tools free.

None of these changes our calculations, rankings or recommendations. Paid placement never changes the math.

Methodology

Calculators use public data from energy agencies and research bodies, such as the US Department of Energy and EPA, ENERGY STAR, NOAA, NASA, Our World in Data and national grid emission factors, combined with the inputs you provide. Each tool explains its formulas and assumptions at the bottom of its page. Defaults are typical values for each country and should be replaced with your own bill figures for the best estimate.

Estimates are for comparing options. Only a site survey and a written quote can tell you the exact price and output for your home.

Editorial standards

  • Claims are sourced to primary data where possible: agencies, regulators, peer-reviewed studies and official program pages.
  • Every guide shows the date it was last updated. When a program or price changes, we update the guide.
  • We correct mistakes quickly and openly. Spot one? Tell us.
  • We do not write about products we have been paid to praise. Sponsored guides are labelled and held to the same standards.
