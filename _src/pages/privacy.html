Privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026.

This policy explains what information Hello.Green (“we”, “us”) collects when you use hello.green, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (quote requests, newsletter sign-ups, contest entries, job applications, partner applications, support pledges or contact messages) we receive the information you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, postal code and project details. We use it only to respond to you and provide the service you asked for.

Quote requests: if you ask for quotes and give consent, we share your request with the number of vetted partner installers you chose (up to three) so they can contact you by your preferred method. You can withdraw consent at any time through our contact page, and we will ask partners to delete your details.

Forms are delivered to us by a third-party form-processing service, which processes submissions on our behalf to send them to our inbox.

Information collected automatically

Advertising. We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and learn more at how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through a consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies at aboutads.info.

Local storage. Our calculators, challenge tracker and preferences (such as dark mode) save your inputs in your browser’s local storage so they are there when you return. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Clear it any time through your browser settings.

Embedded videos. Videos use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Once you do, YouTube’s privacy policy applies.

Server logs. Our hosting provider may record standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability.

We do not run our own analytics trackers and we do not sell personal information.

How long we keep information

We keep form submissions as long as needed to deal with your request and meet legal obligations, generally no longer than 24 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe from any email.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR (EU/UK), the CCPA/CPRA (California), PIPEDA and Québec’s Law 25 (Canada) and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to withdraw consent and to object to certain processing. To make a request, use our contact page and choose “Withdraw my quote request or delete my data”. We will respond within the time the law requires. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Hello.Green is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Contests are open only to adults.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect and who can access it.

International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact page.
