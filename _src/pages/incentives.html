Green home incentives by country

What is open, what has closed and where to check the details. Programs change often, so always confirm with the official page before you sign a contract. Last checked {{last_checked}}.

How to use this page

Find your country, note the programs that fit your project, then ask installers in your quotes which ones they will apply for on your behalf. Many grants are “installer-led”: the installer handles the paperwork and takes the grant off your invoice.

United States

Residential Clean Energy Credit (Section 25D) Ended

The 30% federal credit for homeowner-owned solar, batteries, geothermal heat pumps and small wind ended for expenditures after 31 December 2025, under the law signed on 4 July 2025. Systems completed in 2025 can still be claimed on 2025 returns, and unused credit can be carried forward. IRS FAQ on the changes

Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit (Section 25C) Ended

The credit for heat pumps, insulation, windows, doors and home energy audits ended for property placed in service after 31 December 2025.

Clean vehicle credits (30D, 25E) Ended

The new and used EV tax credits are not available for vehicles acquired after 30 September 2025. IRS clean vehicle credits

Solar leases and power purchase agreements Check with installer

When a company owns the system, it may still claim a business clean-energy credit on qualifying residential systems, subject to construction and placed-in-service deadlines. Ask lease and PPA providers how this affects your rate.

State, utility and local programs Varies by area

Many states, utilities and cities offer rebates for solar, batteries, heat pumps, heat pump water heaters, insulation and EV chargers, and some run income-qualified programs that cover most of the cost. Search by ZIP code:

  • DSIRE: the database of state incentives for renewables and efficiency
  • ENERGY STAR Rebate Finder: utility rebates on certified products
  • Rewiring America incentives calculator

Canada

Canada Greener Homes Affordability Program Open in participating provinces

No-cost home retrofits, such as insulation and heat pumps, for low-to-median-income homeowners and tenants, delivered directly through provincial and territorial partners. Natural Resources Canada

Canada Greener Homes Grant Closed to new applicants

The grant of up to $5,000 plus up to $600 for EnerGuide evaluations supported over 400,000 completed retrofits. It no longer accepts new applications; existing applications are being processed.

Canada Greener Homes Loan Check current intake

Interest-free loans of up to $40,000 over 10 years for retrofits recommended by an energy advisor. Confirm availability on the Greener Homes page before starting work, because work begun before applying is ineligible.

Provincial and utility programs Varies by province

Québec, Ontario, British Columbia, Nova Scotia and other provinces run heat pump, insulation and efficiency rebates through governments and utilities. Start with your provincial energy efficiency agency or utility: for example Hydro-Québec, Save on Energy (Ontario) and CleanBC Better Homes.

United Kingdom

Boiler Upgrade Scheme (England and Wales) Open

  • £7,500 towards an air-to-water or ground source heat pump
  • £2,500 towards an air-to-air heat pump
  • £5,000 towards a biomass boiler (limited circumstances)
  • £9,000 for air-to-water or ground source heat pumps in eligible off-gas-grid homes, from 21 July 2026 until 31 March 2027

Your MCS-certified installer applies on your behalf and deducts the grant from your bill. GOV.UK: Boiler Upgrade Scheme

Scotland: Home Energy Scotland grants and loans Check current offer

Scotland runs its own grant and interest-free loan scheme for heat pumps, insulation and other upgrades, with free impartial advice. Home Energy Scotland

Smart Export Guarantee Open

Larger electricity suppliers must offer a tariff that pays you for solar power you export. Rates differ a lot between suppliers, so shop around.

India

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana Open

Central financial assistance for residential rooftop solar: ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third kW, capped at ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW or more. Households apply and choose a registered vendor on the national portal, and collateral-free loans are available through public sector banks. pmsuryaghar.gov.in

State net metering and DISCOM schemes Varies by state

Net metering rules and any state top-ups are set by your state regulator and distribution company. Ask your vendor to confirm the current rules for your DISCOM.

Australia

Small-scale Renewable Energy Scheme Open, declining yearly

Small-scale technology certificates reduce the upfront price of rooftop solar, solar water heaters and heat pump water heaters. Installers usually apply the discount at the point of sale. Clean Energy Regulator

Cheaper Home Batteries Program Open

Since July 2025, eligible small-scale home batteries installed with solar earn certificates that cut the upfront price. Check the current discount with your installer or the Clean Energy Regulator.

State programs Varies by state

States and territories run their own rebates, interest-free loans and energy upgrade programs. Start at energy.gov.au.

Everywhere else

Most countries in Europe and many elsewhere offer some mix of heat pump grants, solar feed-in or net metering, efficiency loans and EV incentives. Search your national energy agency, your utility and your city. Our calculators work in any currency: change the defaults to your local prices.
