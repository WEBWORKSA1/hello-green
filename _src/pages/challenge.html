    Why small changes are worth it

    Habits are how bigger decisions start. Households that track their energy for a month usually find one or two surprises, such as a second fridge, a water heater set too hot or a draft they had stopped noticing, and those surprises pay for themselves.

    The challenge is ordered from easiest to most involved. By the last week you will be pricing the upgrades that make the largest difference: insulation, a heat pump, solar or an EV.
