    Assumptions

    Gas car fuel cost is distance times fuel use times price. EV cost splits charging between home and public stations at the share you choose; we add 10% for charging losses. Emissions use 2.31 kg CO₂ per litre of gasoline, 2.68 kg per litre of diesel, and your country’s average grid factor for electricity. The EV figure covers driving only; battery manufacturing adds emissions up front, which lifecycle studies find are usually paid back within the first two years of driving.

    Maintenance defaults reflect that EVs have no oil changes, fewer moving parts and less brake wear thanks to regenerative braking. Tyres and insurance can cost more, so adjust if you have quotes.
