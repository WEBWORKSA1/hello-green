    Three rules that fix most recycling mistakes

    1. Empty, clean enough, dry. A quick rinse is enough. Food left in containers spoils paper and cardboard in the same load.
    2. No bags, no tanglers. Plastic bags, hoses, cables and string wrap around sorting machinery and shut lines down. Take bags back to store drop-offs.
    3. When in doubt, leave it out. “Wish-cycling” items that are not accepted raises costs and can send whole loads to landfill.
