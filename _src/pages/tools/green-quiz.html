    How the score works

    Each answer scores from 0 to 3 points, and the total is scaled to 100. The questions are weighted toward the choices that matter most for household emissions in most countries: heating, electricity, travel and food. A high score does not mean a zero footprint, and a low one is simply a list of opportunities, most of which also save money.

    Levels: Seedling (under 40), Sprout (40–59), Sapling (60–74), Grove (75–89) and Old-growth (90 and above). Take the quiz again after a month of changes and compare.
