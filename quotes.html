Quote questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Requesting quotes is free and there is no obligation. Partner installers pay Hello.Green for introductions, which is how we keep the site free.

How are installers vetted?

Partners must hold the licences and insurance their region requires, have verifiable reviews and agree to our contact rules. We remove partners who break those rules.

I rent. Can I still ask?

Yes. Many upgrades, such as portable heat pumps, plug-in solar where allowed, and smart thermostats, work for renters. For permanent work we note that landlord approval is needed.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. Send a withdrawal request from the contact page and we will ask every matched partner to delete your details.
