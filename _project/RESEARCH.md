## Patterns that repeat across the top performers

1. **Calculator → quote.** The highest-intent pages are calculators that show a personal number and then offer real quotes (SolarReviews, EnergySage, Solar.com, NRG).
2. **ZIP/postal code first, contact details last.** Every lead-gen leader asks the lowest-friction question first (Modernize, EnergySage).
3. **Control and trust copy.** "Choose how many pros", "No sales calls", "Free, no obligation", "vetted installers".
4. **Several revenue lines at once.** Ads + lead fees + sponsors + donations + newsletters (Grist, Electrek, CleanTechnica).
5. **Live, self-updating proof.** Counters and fresh feeds make a site feel alive (Ecosia, OWID, news sites).
6. **Transparent giving.** Preset amounts, monthly option, impact framing, business tier (One Tree Planted).
7. **Engagement loops.** Quizzes, challenges, giveaways tied to newsletter growth (EARTHDAY.ORG, Inhabitat).

## Hello.Green positioning

The gap: news sites have audience but weak tools; lead-gen sites have tools but thin, salesy content; nonprofits have trust but no monetisation. Hello.Green combines **original money-saving tools + plain-English guides + an honest quote marketplace**, with ads, lead fees, sponsorships and supporter funding.
