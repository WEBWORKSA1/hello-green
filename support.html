Where the money goes

Our target split for supporter funds:

  • 35% operations: hosting, data feeds, tools and fact-checking
  • 25% promotion and marketing to reach more households
  • 25% hiring writers, video makers and developers
  • 15% contest prizes and community events

Hello.Green is an independent business, not a registered charity, so contributions are not tax-deductible. Supporters get our thanks, early access to new tools and a say in what we build next.
