Help households go green, and get paid for it

We are a small, remote team building the most useful green-home site on the web. Most roles are freelance or contract to start, paid per project or monthly, open to people anywhere.

Open roles

  • Home energy writer

    Freelance, per article

    You understand heat pumps, solar, insulation or building science and can explain them to a homeowner in plain English. Every claim sourced; every guide ends in a decision.

  • Low-waste and lifestyle writer

    Freelance, per article

    Practical, money-saving guides on food waste, composting, repair culture and greenwashing. Warm, specific and never preachy.

  • Video creator and editor

    Freelance, per video

    Short explainers and real-home walkthroughs for YouTube, Shorts, Reels and TikTok. You can script, shoot or edit, ideally all three.

  • Community and social lead

    Part-time contract

    Run our social channels, newsletter community and contests. Turn reader stories into content people share.

  • SEO and growth marketer

    Contract

    Grow organic traffic to our tools and guides, plan landing pages by region, and improve quote conversion without hurting trust.

  • Partnerships and sponsorship sales

    Commission-based

    Sign installers to our quote network and brands to sponsorships. Experience in solar, HVAC or home-services sales is a strong plus.

  • Front-end developer

    Contract

    Build fast, accessible calculators in plain JavaScript, improve data pipelines and keep the site light enough to load on any phone.

  • Volunteer climate ambassador

    Volunteer

    Share tools in your community, report new local incentives and help us test features. Ambassadors get early access and credit on the site.
